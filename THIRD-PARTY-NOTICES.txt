React Bits — David Haz

TiltedPortrait and Magnetic in src/components/Interactions.tsx adapt the
pointer-position interaction patterns from the following source files:
https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits/blob/main/src/content/Components/TiltedCard/TiltedCard.jsx
https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits/blob/main/src/content/Animations/Magnet/Magnet.jsx

Adaptations include TypeScript props, local pointer events, limited movement,
Motion springs, reduced-motion and coarse-pointer support, and removal of the
original tooltip and mobile warning. Reveal is an original Motion composition.

The complete upstream source license follows verbatim:
https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits/blob/main/LICENSE.md
