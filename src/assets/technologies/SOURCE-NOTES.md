# Technology catalog assets

Local assets used by the portfolio's technology catalog in `src/components/TechnologyCatalog.tsx`.

Downloaded on 2026-10-22 from fixed upstream versions. All SVGs are preserved byte-for-byte: no geometry, colors, metadata, or formatting were changed. No script elements were present.

Devicon original variants preserve upstream brand colors for React, Angular, TypeScript, Java, Spring, C#, .NET Core, Python, FastAPI, and PostgreSQL. Next.js retains its upstream monochrome plain variant; LangChain retains its upstream monochrome Simple Icons SVG. No colors have been invented or applied to the source files.
